How it works

Three steps. About ten minutes total.

Recognise

Ten questions, ninety seconds. You get a score, a profile, and a breakdown showing which part of your phone use is the problem — because for most people it's one thing, not everything.

Take the check

Manage

Log your real hours each day, set a goal you'd actually hit, and watch the chart. Almost everyone guesses about half their real number. Seeing it is the hard bit.

Open my dashboard

Reduce

Twelve specific swaps, a focus timer, a seven day challenge and a wind-down plan. Small and concrete. Nothing here tells you to delete anything.

Open the toolkit

Nothing you type here leaves your device.

No account, no login, no server, no analytics, no cookies. Everything is stored in your own browser, and there's a delete everything button on the dashboard. No one can see any of your data.
