Addiction buster

Things that actually work.

None of these are "use your phone less". They're specific, they take under five minutes to set up, and most of them work by changing your setup rather than relying on willpower — because willpower at 11:30pm is not a real thing.

Swap cards

Find the habit. Flip the card. Try the swap tonight.

Focus timer

25 on, 5 off.

Put your phone in another room — not face-down on the desk, actually out of the room. Work for 25 minutes, then take 5. Knowing a break is coming is what stops the itch; "never check it" has never worked for anyone.

The seven-day challenge

One small thing a day.

Do them in order or don't. They don't have to be consecutive days. Your progress saves automatically.

Why this works

Big resolutions fail because they need you to be a different person by Monday. Each of these takes under ten minutes and changes one part of your environment. Environment beats intention — every time.

Wind-down builder

Work backwards from bed.

The blue-light thing is the small problem. The real one is that scrolling keeps your brain switched on and quietly pushes your bedtime later and later. Set your target bedtime and this builds the ninety minutes before it.

Tip: print this page and stick the timeline on your wall. It works better on paper than on a screen — which is sort of the whole point.

Tonight's plan

  • 9:00pmLast scroll
    Say what you are doing to whoever you are talking to, then put it down. Ending on your own terms is the whole point.
  • 9:30pmPhone goes out
    Onto its charger, outside the bedroom. This one step does more than every other step combined.
  • 9:45pmSomething analogue
    Shower, food, tidy, guitar, stretch, chat. Anything where your hands are busy and nothing is scrolling.
  • 10:05pmTomorrow, on paper
    Write down the three things you have to do tomorrow. Half of lying awake is your brain refusing to let go of a list.
  • 10:15pmRead something
    Paper if you have it. Ten pages is plenty — it is about the transition, not the reading.
  • 10:30pmLights out
    Actual bedtime. If you are still awake in 20 minutes, that is normal. Do not go and get the phone.
